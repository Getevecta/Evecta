# 1. Product Overview

## What problem does Evecta solve?

Evecta is an AI-powered lead generation and outreach platform. It combines lead discovery, lead
management, AI-personalized email writing, spam checking, mailbox integration, campaign
management, and campaign insights into a single workflow. Its core features are:

- **AI Lead Generation:** Users describe the businesses they want to target by industry, market,
  location, or customer profile, and Evecta generates relevant business prospects.
- **Permanent Search History:** Every lead search is permanently stored, including saved and
  discarded results. Users can return to previous searches and save prospects to the Lead
  Database later.
- **Lead Database:** Users can save selected prospects to the Lead Database. Unlike Search
  History, leads here can be deleted, so users can maintain a clean list of active prospects.
- **Campaign Creation:** Users can create campaigns directly from Search History, the Lead
  Database, or other lead views without a lengthy setup process.
- **AI-Personalized Email Generation:** Evecta generates a personalized email draft for each lead
  based on the campaign objective and that prospect's specific business context, rather than just
  swapping the recipient's name.
- **Spam Check:** Before sending, Evecta can analyze a message for spam and deliverability risks
  and flag wording that may hurt inbox placement.
- **Mailbox Integration:** Users can connect their own email account or use Evecta's warmed
  mailboxes to send campaign emails.
- **Campaign Management:** Users can create campaigns, select leads, review AI-generated drafts,
  schedule outreach, and monitor activity from within Evecta.
- **Unified Inbox:** When prospects reply, users can read and respond to conversations directly
  within Evecta without switching to another email application.
- **Campaign Insights:** Evecta shows engagement data such as email opens, replies, bounces,
  unopened emails, and overall campaign activity, helping users evaluate performance and decide
  on appropriate manual next steps.
- **Credits and Packages:** Users can subscribe to monthly packages and purchase additional
  credits when they need more usage without upgrading their whole subscription.

**Typical workflow:** describe the target audience, generate leads, review Search History, save
selected leads to the Lead Database, create a campaign, generate personalized emails, run spam
checks, send through a connected mailbox, track engagement, manage replies in the Unified Inbox,
and use Campaign Insights to improve future campaigns.

## Who are the target users and customers?

Evecta is built for businesses and professionals who rely on outbound sales and want a faster,
smarter way to find customers and manage outreach. It is ideal for:

- **Startup Founders:** Founders looking to acquire their first customers.
- **Small & Medium Businesses (SMBs):** Businesses that want to grow without building a large
  sales team.
- **Sales Teams & Business Development Representatives (BDRs):** Teams generating leads and
  managing outbound campaigns.

Whether a user is looking for their first client or running thousands of outreach emails a month,
Evecta keeps the entire process in one place.

## Is Evecta only a lead-generation tool?

No. Lead generation is only the first part of Evecta. It also provides lead management,
AI-personalized email writing, automatic spam checking, mailbox integration, warmed sending
addresses, campaign creation, campaign scheduling, reply management, and campaign-level insights.

# 2. Lead Generation

## How do I generate leads in Evecta?

Enter a prompt describing the type of businesses you want to reach — industry, location, business
type, service requirement, target market, or other relevant characteristics. Evecta uses this
intent to generate a matching list of businesses.

## What should I include in a lead-generation prompt?

A strong prompt should include:

- The type of business you want to find
- The industry or service category
- The preferred location
- The type of customer you want to reach
- Any important business characteristics

Example: "Find hotels in Kathmandu." A more specific prompt generally produces more relevant
results.

## Can I search for businesses by industry?

Yes — hotels, restaurants, schools, software companies, healthcare businesses, retailers,
manufacturers, professional service providers, and more.

## Can I search for businesses by location?

Yes, by city. Result quality and availability may depend on how much business data exists for
that location.

## Can I combine industry and location in one search?

Yes. Example: "Find digital marketing agencies in Sydney that work with real-estate companies."

## How does Evecta decide which businesses are relevant?

Evecta uses the meaning and intent of the prompt to identify businesses matching the requested
industry, location, business type, or customer profile. Relevance depends partly on how clearly
the target audience is described.

## What should I do if the generated leads are not relevant?

Refine the prompt with more specific information: exact industry, preferred location, business
size, type of service offered, the type of problem the target business may have, and businesses
that should be excluded.

## Are all generated leads automatically added to the Lead Database?

No. Generated leads first remain in Search History; the user decides which are useful and saves
only those into the Lead Database.

## Can I generate leads without immediately creating a campaign?

Yes. Leads can be reviewed first, saved to the Lead Database, or revisited later via Search
History.

## Can I create a campaign directly from generated leads?

Yes — from generated search results, Search History, or the Lead Database.

# 3. Search History & Lead Database

## What is Search History in Evecta?

A permanent record of every lead search generated by the user, including results that were saved,
discarded, or not selected.

## Are all previous lead searches stored in Search History?

Yes — every search remains available so the user can return to it later.

## Does Search History include discarded leads?

Yes. Discarded or unselected leads remain visible in the original search, so users can reconsider
them later without re-running the search.

## Can I recover a lead that I previously discarded?

Yes — open the relevant Search History, locate the lead, and save it to the Lead Database.
Discarding does not permanently remove it.

## Can Search History be deleted?

No. Search History is retained and cannot be deleted by the user, so previously generated results
remain recoverable.

## Why is Search History permanent?

It prevents users from losing generated leads. A lead that isn't useful today may become relevant
for a future service, campaign, location, or target market.

## Can I create a campaign from Search History?

Yes. Open the relevant search, select the leads you want, and create a campaign from that
selection.

## Will deleting a lead from the Lead Database remove it from Search History?

No. Deleting removes the lead from the active saved-lead list only. Search History is permanent,
so the original result remains there and the lead can be saved again later.

## Can I delete leads from the Lead Database?

Yes. Deleting a saved lead does not remove the original result from Search History.

## Can I save a lead from Search History to the Lead Database?

Yes — open the relevant search, select the lead, and save it.

## Can I create a campaign from the Lead Database?

Yes. Select the saved leads you want to target and create a campaign from that selection.

# 4. AI-Personalized Emails

## How does Evecta generate personalized emails?

The user provides the campaign's purpose/intent. Evecta uses that intent, plus available
information about each lead, to generate a separate outreach email per prospect.

## Does Evecta send the same email to every lead?

No. Each lead gets an individual draft; all emails follow the same campaign objective but are
adjusted to the particular prospect and available business information.

## What does campaign intent mean?

The main goal and direction of the outreach — what's being offered, who the user wants to reach,
why the offer may be relevant, what action the recipient should take, and what tone the email
should use.

## Why is campaign intent important?

It guides the AI, helping produce messages that stay focused on the same objective while still
being personalized per lead.

## What information should I include in the campaign intent?

Include:

- The product or service being offered
- The target audience
- The main customer problem
- The value or benefit being presented
- The preferred tone
- The desired call to action

Example: "Introduce our website redesign service to hotels in Kathmandu. Focus on improving
direct bookings and mobile experience. Keep the tone professional and invite them to a short
consultation."

## Are AI-generated emails different for every lead?

Yes — separate content per prospect, aligned to the same campaign intent.

## Can I review an AI-generated email before sending?

Yes. Users should confirm content, offer, business details, tone, and call to action before
sending.

## Can I edit an AI-generated email?

Yes. Open the draft in the campaign email review step and edit it directly. Editing returns the
email to a pending state so the changed version can be re-checked and approved before sending.

## Can I regenerate an AI-generated email?

Yes. Improving the campaign intent before regenerating typically produces a more relevant result.
Regeneration creates a new version for the selected email, which should be reviewed again.

## Can I change the tone of the email?

Yes — include the preferred tone (professional, friendly, concise, conversational, formal,
consultative, etc.) in the campaign intent. Available tones depend on your plan; see
*Which AI email tones can I access on my plan?*

## Can I create different campaigns for different services?

Yes — for different products, services, industries, locations, offers, or target audiences.

# 5. Campaign Management

## How do I create a campaign in Evecta?

The general workflow is:

1. Select leads from Search History, generated results, or the Lead Database.
2. Create a new campaign.
3. Add the campaign name and intent.
4. Choose the sending mailbox.
5. Generate personalized emails.
6. Review the drafts.
7. Review the Spam Check results.
8. Schedule or launch the campaign.
9. Track campaign activity through Campaign Insights.

## Can I create campaigns from different sections?

Yes — including Search History and the Lead Database.

## Can I create unlimited campaigns?

Yes. The number of leads processed, emails sent, mailboxes available, or other usage may still
depend on credits, sending capacity, and package conditions.

## Does unlimited campaigns mean unlimited emails?

No. Unlimited campaigns means unlimited campaign creation; total emails/leads processed depends
on available credits, mailbox limits, sending limits, or subscription conditions.

## Can I create separate campaigns for different industries?

Yes — recommended, since different audiences usually need different campaign intents and
messaging.

## Can I review the emails before launching a campaign?

Yes. All drafts can be read, edited, approved, or rejected in the campaign email review step
before launch.

## Can I edit an email inside a campaign?

Yes. Editing returns that email to a pending state so the changed version can be re-checked and
approved before sending.

## Can I choose the mailbox used for a campaign?

Yes — an available connected mailbox or an eligible Evecta warmed email address.

## Can I use a different mailbox for each campaign?

Yes, provided the required mailboxes are connected or available under the account.

## Can I pause or stop a campaign once it has launched?

No. Once a campaign is launched, it cannot be paused or stopped.

## Can I duplicate an existing campaign?

No, there is currently no option to duplicate an existing campaign.

## Can I add leads after a campaign has started?

Leads can be added while the campaign is still in Draft. Once the campaign is launched, no new
leads can be added — if a lead was missed after launch, a new campaign needs to be created for it.

## Can I remove leads before launching a campaign?

Yes, leads can be removed at any point while the campaign is still in Draft.

## Can I change a campaign after launch?

No. Draft editing is not available after launch: leads cannot be added, launched campaigns cannot
be deleted, and the campaign cannot be paused or stopped. Create a new Draft campaign for
materially different recipients or outreach.

## What are the actual campaign statuses?

Evecta stores only **Draft**, **Ongoing**, and **Completed**.

- **Draft** — setup and review are still in progress; the campaign has not been launched.
- **Ongoing** — the campaign was launched and still has sending work or active processing.
- **Completed** — campaign sending has finished.

"Scheduled" and "launched" describe actions or workflow moments; they are not stored campaign
statuses.

## Does my plan restrict AI email tone options?

Yes. In the "Generate with AI" draft flow, tone options are grouped by plan tier:

- **Free & Starter:** Professional, Friendly
- **Growth:** adds Concise, Persuasive, Consultative
- **Scale:** adds Research-Based, Problem-Focused, Strategic

Tones above the user's current plan appear greyed out with a lock icon and an "Upgrade to Unlock"
badge.

## Which AI email tones can I access on my plan?

Free and Starter users have access to the Professional and Friendly tones. Growth adds Concise,
Persuasive, and Consultative. Scale adds Research-Based, Problem-Focused, and Strategic. Higher-
tier tone groups remain locked according to the plan shown in the product.

## What pre-built templates are available under "Browse Templates"?

Three ready-made templates: **Value Proposition**, **Network Outreach**, and **Professional** —
usable as an alternative to AI-generated drafts.

## Can I manage custom email templates?

Yes. In addition to the three ready-made templates, users can create, edit, delete, categorize,
and favorite custom templates for reuse.

## Can I delete a campaign?

Drafted campaigns can be deleted, but launched campaigns cannot be deleted.

## What is the AI email review workflow?

Generate personalized per-lead drafts, run Spam Check, review and edit the messages, and
regenerate an individual email when needed. Approve emails that are ready and reject those that
should not be sent. If generation is interrupted, it can be resumed; generated emails can also be
discarded when the user wants to restart the preparation step.

## When is a campaign ready to launch?

It is ready when generation has finished, the required Spam Checks and compliance checks are
complete, the emails intended for sending are approved, the selected mailbox is connected and
eligible, and sufficient Outreach credits and sending capacity are available. Rejected emails are
not sent.

## Can I send a test email before launching?

Yes. From the campaign preview/review flow, send a personalized test version to your own email
address before launch. Use it to verify personalization, subject, layout, links, sender details,
and the call to action. A test does not launch the campaign.

# 6. Spam Check

## What is Evecta's Spam Check?

It analyzes email content before sending and identifies language or patterns that may increase
the risk of the email being treated as spam.

## Is the Spam Check automatic?

Emails generated for outreach go through Evecta's Spam Check as part of the preparation workflow.

## What does the Spam Check analyze?

It analyzes:

- Overly promotional language
- Spam-triggering words
- Excessive punctuation
- Misleading or exaggerated claims
- Poor content structure
- Suspicious links or formatting
- Other content-related deliverability risks

## Can I edit an email after the Spam Check?

Yes — and the email should be checked again after significant changes. Editing returns the email
to a pending state so the changed version is re-checked before approval.

## What should I do if an email has a high spam risk?

Review the identified issues and revise the email — remove exaggerated claims, excessive sales
language, unnecessary links, repeated punctuation, or misleading wording.

## Does passing the Spam Check guarantee inbox delivery?

No. It reduces content-related risk but does not guarantee inbox placement. Delivery also depends
on mailbox reputation, domain configuration, sending volume, recipient provider, bounce rate,
recipient engagement, and other factors.

## Why can an email still end up in spam after passing the Spam Check?

Because Spam Check focuses mainly on content. Other causes include poor sender reputation, a new
or untrusted mailbox, missing SPF/DKIM/DMARC records, high sending volume, high bounce or
complaint rates, recipient-server filtering, or low previous engagement.

## Does Evecta guarantee email replies?

No. Evecta helps improve targeting, personalization, sending preparation, and campaign tracking,
but cannot guarantee opens, replies, meetings, or sales.

# 7. Mailbox Integration

## Can I connect my own mailbox to Evecta?

Yes.

## Why should I connect my mailbox?

It lets the user send campaigns from their chosen email account, receive replies, view
conversations, and respond from inside Evecta.

## How many mailboxes can I connect?

Unlimited, for now.

## Which mailbox providers and connection types are supported?

Evecta supports a Google/Gmail connection flow and a separate manual connection for other
providers using IMAP for incoming mail and SMTP for outgoing mail.

## How do I choose the mailbox used for a campaign?

During campaign creation/setup, select one of the available connected or warmed mailboxes.

## Can I send campaigns without connecting my own mailbox?

Yes — Evecta's built-in warmed email addresses are available on all packages and can be used
instead. How much you can send through them is bounded by your package's Outreach credit
allowance.

## Why is my mailbox not connecting?

Possible causes: incorrect login details, incorrect SMTP/IMAP settings, missing permissions,
two-factor authentication, app-password requirements, provider security restrictions, disabled
third-party access, or expired authorization. Verify provider settings and reconnect. Detailed
steps are in section 15.

## What should I do if my mailbox becomes disconnected?

Reconnect from Email Accounts using the same connection type. If the problem continues, confirm
credentials, permissions, app password, provider restrictions, and authorization status.

# 8. Warmed Email Addresses

## What are Evecta's built-in warmed email addresses?

Sending mailboxes that have undergone a gradual warm-up process before being used for outreach,
helping establish more natural sending activity and healthier sender reputation than a completely
new address.

## Why should I use a warmed email address?

A newly created mailbox may have little or no sender reputation; a properly warmed mailbox can
reduce some deliverability risks tied to sending outreach from a brand-new address.

## Does a warmed mailbox guarantee inbox delivery?

No. Email quality, targeting, sending behaviour, domain setup, bounce rate, complaints, and
recipient engagement still affect deliverability.

## Are warmed email addresses included in every plan?

Yes, warmed email addresses are available in all packages. Each package comes with a different
number of leads and outreach credits, so how much warmed-mailbox outreach a user can send depends
on their package's credit allowance.

## Can I choose between my own mailbox and a warmed mailbox?

Yes.

## When should I use my own mailbox?

When brand identity matters, the recipient should recognize the company domain, existing business
communication already happens through that mailbox, or the mailbox has a healthy sending
reputation.

## When should I use a warmed mailbox?

When the user doesn't want to send outreach from their primary mailbox, the existing mailbox is
new or unprepared for outreach, a separate address is wanted for campaigns, or a ready-to-use
sending option is needed.

# 9. Unified Inbox & Replies

## Where can I see replies from prospects?

Inside Evecta's Unified Inbox.

## Can I reply to a prospect directly from Evecta?

Yes, using the connected mailbox.

## Do I need to open my external email account to respond?

No — campaign-related replies can be viewed and answered through the Unified Inbox.

## How do I know which campaign a reply belongs to?

Replies are linked with the relevant lead and campaign so the user has context before responding.

## How do I know which lead sent the reply?

The reply is connected to the relevant recipient information inside the Unified Inbox.

## What happens when a prospect replies?

The reply appears inside Evecta, allowing the user to review the conversation and respond
directly.

## What can I do in the Unified Inbox?

Open a conversation to see its full thread, mark conversations as read, star important
conversations, view the related campaign and lead context, and reply from inside Evecta using the
connected mailbox.

## Does Evecta currently send automated follow-up sequences?

No. Automated follow-ups and sequencing are a future update, not a currently available feature.
Users can continue the conversation manually by replying from the Unified Inbox.

## Can multiple team members manage replies?

Yes. There is currently no role-based restriction — whoever has access to the account that
connected a given mailbox can use and manage that mailbox's inbox.

# 10. Campaign Insights

## What are Campaign Insights?

Campaign-level and lead-level information about how recipients interacted with the outreach — who
opened, who replied, and who did not open.

## What information can I see for each campaign?

Each campaign has its own insights page showing the full list of leads along with their status
(sent, opened, replied). It also shows how many emails were sent and on which day, along with
total opens and total replies for the campaign.

## How do I see who opened an email?

Open the relevant campaign and review the recipient activity / campaign insights section.

## How do I see who replied?

Open the relevant campaign and filter/review recipients with replied status, or check the Unified
Inbox.

## How do I see which recipients did not open the email?

Open campaign insights and review recipients marked as not opened / unopened.

## Can I view activity for each lead?

Yes. Each lead in the campaign insights table shows its own status and activity.

## What does "Sent" status mean?

Evecta submitted the email through the selected sending mailbox — it does not necessarily mean
the recipient opened, read, or replied.

## What does "Opened" status mean?

Email-open tracking detected the email was opened. Not always fully accurate — some providers
block tracking, others load it automatically.

## What does "Replied" status mean?

A response from the recipient was detected and connected to the relevant campaign/conversation.

## What does "Not opened" status mean?

No open event has been detected. The email may genuinely be unopened, or the provider may have
blocked open tracking.

## Are email-open results always accurate?

No — treat open tracking as a useful indicator, not an exact measurement. Some clients block
tracking pixels; some privacy features auto-load images and create false opens.

## What should I do when a lead opens but does not reply?

Review the email content, offer, call to action, and relevance. A thoughtful manual reply or
later outreach may be appropriate, but an open should not always be read as strong interest.
Evecta does not currently automate follow-up sequences.

## What should I do when a lead does not open the email?

Review subject line, sending time, sender name, mailbox reputation, deliverability, and target
relevance. A not-opened status should not always be interpreted as rejection. Any follow-up must
currently be handled manually.

## How can Campaign Insights improve future campaigns?

They help identify which audiences engage more, which campaign intents perform better, which
email approaches receive replies, and which subject lines may need improvement. Use these signals
to plan appropriate manual next steps; automated follow-up sequences are not currently available.

## Can I compare campaigns?

Yes. Each campaign has its own insights page, so results can be reviewed side by side.

## Can I export campaign insights?

No, campaign insights currently cannot be exported.

## Does Evecta show a sending schedule for an active campaign?

Yes. Each launched campaign's insights page shows a "Daily Outreach Rate" (emails/day from the
connected mailbox), a weekly bar chart (Sent, Open Rate, Reply Rate, Bounce Rate), an estimated
completion date, and a per-lead status table for all leads in that campaign.

## Why does Campaign Insights appear delayed?

Sending, delivery events, opens, bounces, and replies are processed asynchronously. Refresh after
allowing processing time. Open tracking may be blocked or auto-triggered by recipient privacy
features, so it is not exact.

# 12. Credits & Billing

## How do Evecta credits work?

Evecta uses credits for specific product actions. The exact cost is shown before or when the
action is performed.

## Exactly how are credits calculated?

Credit use is action-based and split into three independent pools:

| Action | Cost |
|---|---|
| Generating one lead | 1 Lead credit |
| One AI email draft | 1 Email Generation credit |
| One Spam Check | 1 Email Generation credit |
| Sending one email | 1 Outreach credit |

The pools are independent — having credits in one pool does not satisfy an action that spends
another pool.

## Is one lead equal to one credit?

Yes. Finding one lead uses 1 Lead credit.

## Are credits deducted again when I reopen an existing lead?

No, reopening a previously found or saved lead does not deduct another credit.

## Do AI email generation and Spam Check use credits?

Yes. Each AI-generated email draft costs 1 Email Generation credit, and each Spam Check costs 1
Email Generation credit. Sending an email costs 1 Outreach credit.

## Where can I see remaining credits and credit history?

Open **Settings > Usage** to review balances and consumption. The interface tracks Lead, Email
Generation, and Outreach pools independently.

## Why does Evecta say I have insufficient credits when I still have credits?

Check the specific pool required by the action. Lead generation uses Lead credits; AI drafts and
Spam Checks use Email Generation credits; sending uses Outreach credits. A positive balance in a
different pool cannot be substituted.

## Do unused credits carry forward?

No. Unused monthly subscription credits expire and do not roll over to the next billing period.

## Do separately purchased top-up credits expire at subscription renewal?

No. Separately purchased top-up credits are stored independently and are not cleared when
subscription credits renew. Subscription allowances follow their own billing-period rules.

## What happens when I run out of credits?

Once credits are finished, the user needs to upgrade their plan or purchase additional credits to
continue using credit-based actions.

## Can I buy additional credits without upgrading my monthly plan?

Yes — additional credits can be purchased separately without changing the monthly subscription
package.

## Can Free users buy top-up credits?

No. Credit top-ups are not available on the Free plan; upgrade to a paid plan first. Paid-plan
top-up ranges and per-purchase caps depend on the selected pool and current plan.

## Why was my top-up amount rejected?

Select at least one credit pool, use an amount within the plan's allowed minimum and maximum, and
use the required increment. The payment total must also meet the payment processor's minimum
charge. Review the options shown in Billing rather than guessing an amount.

## Do I need to upgrade my package to get more credits?

No — credits can be added separately instead of upgrading the whole package. An upgrade may still
be useful for additional recurring features, mailbox allowances, or monthly benefits.

## Are monthly packages the only payment option?

No — Evecta offers monthly packages and also allows separate additional-credit purchases.

## Why did my paid plan or top-up not appear after checkout?

Confirm that checkout completed and was paid, return to Evecta, and refresh Billing/Usage. Do not
repeat payment immediately. If payment succeeded but the plan or credits remain missing, contact
contact@evecta.io with the account email, checkout time, amount, and payment receipt or session
reference. Never send full card details.

## How does subscription cancellation work?

Use the available Billing or customer-portal cancellation flow. A subscription may remain active
until the end of the paid period and show a scheduled cancellation date. If the product says the
subscription is already cancelled, wait until the displayed expiry before attempting a
conflicting subscription action.

## Why can I not open the billing portal?

The account needs an associated payment-customer record. Complete a subscription checkout first
when instructed. Retry a temporary failure once, then contact support if the portal still cannot
be created.

# 13. Security & Privacy

## Does Evecta sell user data?

No. Evecta does not sell user or lead data.

## Who owns the campaigns and email content created in Evecta?

# 14. Current Product Operations & Policies

## How do I import leads from Excel?

Open **Lead Database** and click **Upload Excel**. Select an `.xlsx` or `.xls` workbook.

## Can I upload CSV through Upload Excel?

No. Upload Excel accepts `.xlsx` or `.xls` only. CSV is not accepted by that importer.

## Which columns are required for an Excel lead import?

Every workbook must have a header row with **Company Name** and **Email** columns, and every
non-blank data row must contain both values. Accepted header aliases include company/business
name variations and email/e-mail variations.

## Which Excel columns are optional?

Optional columns are Industry, Contact Number, Address, and Website. Common alternatives such as
Sector, Category, Phone, Mobile, Location, Web, URL, and Website URL are recognized.

## What is the Excel upload limit?

Evecta processes up to 1,000 non-blank data rows per upload. Blank rows are ignored, and rows
beyond the 1,000-row limit are reported and not imported.

## How are duplicates and Excel import errors handled?

A lead whose email already exists in the user's Lead Database is skipped. Repeated email
addresses within the same workbook are also skipped. Rows missing Company Name or Email fail
validation, blank rows are ignored, and the result summary reports imported, duplicate, failed,
blank, and over-limit rows. An empty file, missing required headers, or an unreadable workbook
produces an error.

## What should I do if Excel upload reports zero imported rows?

Confirm that the workbook has a header and non-blank data rows, includes Company Name and Email,
and that both required values exist on every intended row. Review whether every row was a
duplicate, invalid, blank, or beyond the 1,000-row limit. Use the product sample workbook as a
formatting reference.

## What is an email bounce?

A bounce means a recipient's mail system did not accept or deliver the message. Common causes
include an invalid or nonexistent address, a full mailbox, temporary server problems, domain or
DNS issues, provider filtering, sender-reputation problems, or policy restrictions.

## Where can I see Bounce Rate, and what should I do about bounces?

Bounce Rate appears in the campaign insights weekly performance chart. Review bounced recipients,
correct or remove invalid addresses, avoid retrying permanently invalid recipients, improve list
quality, and check mailbox/domain configuration. A high bounce rate can harm sender reputation.

## What should I do with a permanently bounced address?

Correct it only when a verified replacement address is available; otherwise remove or suppress it
from future outreach. Repeatedly sending to permanently invalid recipients damages list quality
and sender reputation.

## Does Evecta add unsubscribe links?

Yes. Campaign email handling supports an unsubscribe link and one-click unsubscribe so recipients
can opt out.

## What happens after a recipient unsubscribes?

The lead is marked opted out/unsubscribed and should not be emailed again. Treat the opt-out as
final unless the recipient later gives valid new consent; do not add that lead to another
outreach campaign.

## What should I do with an unsubscribed lead?

Do not email the lead again through another campaign or mailbox. Keep the opt-out suppression
intact unless valid new consent is obtained outside this workflow. Unsubscribe handling takes
precedence over campaign intent or remaining credits.

## What are the daily sending limit and send interval?

Each user can send up to **50 successful campaign emails per UTC day**, and Evecta spaces sends
by at least **60 seconds**. Failed attempts do not count as successful sends.

## Why did some campaign emails move to the next day, or why is my campaign sending slowly?

Emails are queued to protect sending health and enforce capacity. Once the user reaches 50
successful sends in the current UTC day, remaining queued emails wait for the next UTC day. The
60-second spacing also means a larger campaign is delivered gradually rather than all at once.
Provider restrictions, temporary failures, and mailbox availability can extend completion time
further.

## Can I update my profile and business details?

Yes. Open **Profile/Settings** to update personal and company information such as your name and
business details. Keep this information accurate because available sender and company context can
influence AI-personalized outreach content and signatures.

## How do I contact Evecta support?

Email **contact@evecta.io**. Support is available by email, with a response time within 24 hours.

# 15. Mailbox Setup & Troubleshooting

## Where do I add a new mailbox?

Open **Email Accounts** and choose **Add Email Account**. A mailbox can also be added or selected
during the campaign mailbox-selection step. Choose **Google/Gmail** for the Google authorization
flow, or **Any Provider (IMAP/SMTP)** for manual server configuration.

## How do I connect a Google or Gmail mailbox?

Select Google/Gmail, continue to Google, choose the intended Google account, review the requested
access, and grant the permissions needed by Evecta. After Google returns you to Evecta, confirm
that the mailbox appears in Email Accounts as connected. Google authorization is used instead of
entering the Google password into Evecta.

## Why did the Google/Gmail connection fail?

Confirm that the correct Google account was selected and that the authorization was not
cancelled. Grant all permissions required for sending and mailbox synchronization. If the
connection was previously working, the authorization may have expired or been revoked. Retry the
Google connection; if it still fails, check whether the Google account or organization
administrator blocks third-party access.

## How do I manually connect an SMTP provider or another mailbox provider?

Select **Any Provider (IMAP/SMTP)**. Enter the incoming IMAP host, IMAP port, IMAP username, and
IMAP password. Then enter the outgoing SMTP host, SMTP port, SMTP username, and SMTP password.
Use the exact server, port, encryption, and authentication details supplied by the mailbox
provider. Do not guess settings from another provider.

## What is the difference between IMAP and SMTP?

IMAP is used to read and synchronize incoming messages and replies. SMTP is used to send outgoing
messages. A mailbox can therefore send successfully while reply synchronization fails, or
synchronize incoming mail while outgoing sending fails, if only one side is configured correctly.

## Where can I find my IMAP and SMTP settings?

Check the official help or administrator documentation for the mailbox provider or hosting
company. Obtain the IMAP and SMTP host names, ports, SSL/TLS requirements, username format,
authentication requirements, and whether an app password is required. For a company-managed
mailbox, ask the email administrator.

## Should I use my normal mailbox password or an app password?

Follow the provider's requirements. Accounts protected by two-factor authentication commonly
require a provider-generated app password for manual IMAP/SMTP access. If the provider requires
an app password, the normal login password will fail. Never paste either password into an Evecta
support message.

## Why does Evecta say my IMAP/SMTP username or password is incorrect?

Re-enter the full mailbox address when the provider uses it as the username, verify there are no
copied spaces, and confirm whether an app password is required. Also check that IMAP/SMTP access
is enabled and that the provider has not blocked the sign-in as suspicious. Generate a new app
password when an existing one was revoked or expired.

## Why can Evecta not reach the IMAP or SMTP server?

Check the host name, port, and required SSL/TLS configuration against the provider's current
documentation. The service may also be temporarily unavailable, blocked by a provider policy,
firewall, or organization administrator, or affected by a DNS/network timeout. Retry after
confirming the official settings.

## What should I do if IMAP is disabled?

Enable IMAP access in the provider or administrator settings if the provider allows it. Some
organizations disable IMAP or third-party mailbox access centrally; in that case, the
organization administrator must permit it or provide another supported connection method.

## What should I do if SMTP authentication is disabled?

Enable authenticated SMTP if the provider and organization policy allow it, then verify the SMTP
username, password or app password, port, and encryption method. If an administrator controls the
mailbox, ask them whether authenticated SMTP is permitted.

## How do I confirm that a newly connected mailbox works?

Confirm that the mailbox is listed as connected, select it for a draft campaign, and send a
personalized test email to your own address. Verify the sender, subject, content, links, and
delivery. Then confirm that mailbox synchronization is active so campaign replies can appear in
the Unified Inbox. A successful test email checks sending but does not by itself prove that
incoming reply synchronization works.

## Why can I send email but not see replies in the Unified Inbox?

The SMTP or Google sending connection may be working while incoming synchronization is delayed or
unavailable. Check the IMAP connection or Google authorization, confirm the reply was sent to the
same connected address, and look for provider filtering. Reconnect the mailbox if authorization
or credentials changed. Allow time for synchronization, then contact support if the reply still
does not appear.

## Why can I see incoming mail but campaign emails will not send?

Incoming IMAP synchronization may be working while SMTP sending is failing. Verify SMTP host,
port, encryption, authentication, password or app password, provider sending restrictions,
campaign readiness, Outreach credits, daily capacity, and mailbox connection status.

## What should I do if a connected mailbox becomes disconnected?

Open **Email Accounts** and reconnect it using the same connection type. For Google/Gmail, repeat
Google authorization and grant the required permissions. For manual IMAP/SMTP, verify current
server settings and replace changed or expired credentials. Reconnection may be necessary after a
password change, app-password revocation, security-policy change, or authorization expiry.

## Can I remove a mailbox and add it again?

Yes. Use the mailbox management controls in **Email Accounts** to remove or disconnect the
mailbox, then add it again with the correct Google authorization or IMAP/SMTP settings. Before
removing a mailbox, check whether it is selected by a draft or ongoing campaign so campaign
sending or reply context is not disrupted.

## Why is a connected mailbox not available when I create a campaign?

Refresh the mailbox list and confirm that the account is connected and eligible for sending.
Check whether campaign setup is using the intended sending mode and whether the connection
finished successfully. Reconnect the mailbox if it is marked disconnected; contact support if it
remains missing.

## Does connecting a mailbox guarantee delivery?

No. A valid connection enables sending and synchronization, but delivery still depends on address
quality, sender and domain reputation, SPF/DKIM/DMARC configuration, message content, recipient
filtering, bounce and complaint rates, provider policies, and sending behavior.

## What information should I send to support about a mailbox problem?

Email **contact@evecta.io** with the mailbox provider, connection type (Google or IMAP/SMTP), the
exact non-secret error message, the affected Evecta screen or campaign, and the approximate
failure time and time zone. A screenshot is useful if it contains no credentials or tokens. Never
include passwords, app passwords, authorization codes, or recovery codes.

# 17. Lead Generation, Search History & Lead Database Support

## Why does AI Lead Generation say the message is empty or chat is starting up?

Enter a non-empty targeting request. If chat is starting up, wait briefly and retry once. A
repeated startup or processing failure is a platform issue; preserve the non-secret error and
time and contact support.

## What should I do while lead generation is still processing?

Keep the generation view open or return through the active processing notification when
available. Background enrichment can continue after initial businesses appear. Do not submit
repeated identical searches merely because optional contact data is still processing.

## What happens if I leave the lead-generation page?

The interface may continue polling or allow the result to be reopened when processing completes.
If unsaved results are present, follow the Save or Discard prompt before navigating so the
intended leads are not lost from the active view. Search History retains the original search.

## Why are some lead fields shown as N/A or still processing?

Business discovery and contact enrichment are separate. A business can be found even when an
email, phone, address, industry, or website is unavailable or still being enriched. N/A means the
value was not available; it should not be treated as verified contact data.

## How do I save generated leads?

Select the relevant generated results and save them to the Lead Database. Duplicate rules prevent
the same user from saving the same email repeatedly. If a result is not saved, it remains
available in its Search History entry.

## Why was a generated or imported lead skipped as a duplicate?

Evecta normalizes email addresses and checks the user's existing Lead Database as well as
duplicates within the current import. Review the existing saved lead instead of creating another
copy.

## What can I do with a saved lead?

Open the lead, review its details and campaign context, update editable information, star or
unstar it, select it for a campaign, or delete it from the Lead Database. Deleting the saved lead
does not remove its original Search History result.

## Why can I not find a Lead Database entry?

Clear restrictive search or filters, check the relevant page, and verify whether the lead was
saved rather than only generated. Review Search History for the original result. The lead may
also have been deleted from the active database.

# 18. Campaign Creation, Email Review & Launch Troubleshooting

## Why can I not create a campaign from selected leads?

Confirm that at least one valid saved lead is selected and that the campaign name and required
setup information are complete. If selection came from another view, return to that view and
select the leads again. Refresh once if the selection did not carry into the Draft campaign.

## Why can I not create a campaign?

Confirm at least one lead is selected, required campaign information is complete, a sending
mailbox is available, sufficient credits/usage allowance is available, and the account has the
required permission.

## Why is a campaign still Draft?

Draft is correct until the campaign is launched. Complete lead selection, campaign intent,
mailbox selection, email generation, Spam Check and review, test preview if desired, and launch
readiness. "Scheduled" is not a stored status.

## Why did AI email generation partially fail or stop?

Check the generation status and failed count. Generation can be cancelled, resumed, or discarded.
Resume interrupted work when appropriate, regenerate failed or individual emails, and review
successful drafts. Do not launch until the intended messages are ready and approved.

## What is the difference between cancel, discard, resume, and regenerate?

- **Cancel** stops the current email-generation run when supported.
- **Resume** continues an interrupted generation state.
- **Discard** removes the generated-email set so preparation can restart.
- **Regenerate** creates a new version for a selected email or requested generation scope.

Review the resulting version again after any of these.

## Why was my email not generated?

Possible causes: missing or unclear campaign intent, incomplete lead information, insufficient
Email Generation credits, a temporary AI-processing error, or a network/server issue. Improve the
campaign intent and regenerate.

## Why did editing an email make it require review or Spam Check again?

Editing or regenerating changes the content that was previously reviewed. The email returns to
the appropriate pending/compliance state so the changed version can be checked and approved
before sending.

## Why did the Spam Check not complete?

Possible causes: the email draft was not generated, the content is empty, a temporary processing
issue occurred, the request timed out, or the required credit/feature access is unavailable.
Retry, and contact support if it continues.

## Why is an email rejected or excluded from launch?

Rejected emails are intentionally skipped. Review the content and lead context, edit or regenerate
if the message should be reconsidered, rerun required checks, and approve only the final
acceptable version.

## Why is the campaign not ready to launch?

Open the launch-readiness checklist. Common blockers are missing leads, incomplete generation,
pending email review, failed required checks, no approved emails, disconnected or unselected
mailbox, insufficient Outreach credits, or another incomplete campaign setting.

## Why did the test email fail?

Confirm that the user profile has an email address, the campaign has previewable content, and the
sending setup is available. If the error says the user email is missing, update the profile email
and retry. A test failure does not launch the campaign.

## Why is my campaign not sending?

Check mailbox connection, campaign schedule, available Outreach credits, sending limits, campaign
status, email draft status, Spam Check requirements, and provider restrictions.

## Why is a scheduled campaign delayed, or why does an Ongoing campaign take more than one day?

Evecta sends at least 60 seconds apart and caps each user at 50 successful campaign emails per
UTC day. Remaining jobs stay queued for the next UTC day. Mailbox limits, sending intervals,
provider restrictions, campaign queue processing, temporary failures, or a disconnected mailbox
can also extend completion time.

## Why are Campaign Insights not updating?

Campaign data may take time to appear because sending, opening, and replying events need to be
received and processed. Open tracking can also be blocked by the recipient's email provider.

# 19. Inbox, Compliance, Data Safety & Escalation

## Why is the Inbox empty?

Confirm that a mailbox is connected, the campaign sent from that mailbox, and recipients have
replied to the connected address. Trigger or allow reply synchronization, clear restrictive
filters, and check that replies were not sent elsewhere or filtered by the provider.

## Why is a reply not appearing in the Unified Inbox?

Possible causes: the mailbox is disconnected, mail synchronization is delayed, the response was
sent to a different address, the message was filtered by the mailbox provider, the reply could
not be linked to the campaign, or a temporary sync issue occurred.

## Why can I not open or reply to an Inbox thread?

The thread or lead may no longer be available to the account, the mailbox may be disconnected, or
reply sending may have failed. Refresh, reopen the thread, verify mailbox connection, and retry
without changing the recipient context. Escalate repeated "thread not found," authorization, or
database errors.

## What should I do if data appears under the wrong user or campaign?

Stop the affected workflow and do not send. Record the screen, non-secret identifiers, time, and
what appears incorrect, then email **contact@evecta.io** immediately. Do not modify or delete
records merely to hide a possible account-association problem.

## What is the final support checklist before escalation?

Record the account email, feature and screen, desired action, exact non-secret error, campaign or
lead context, mailbox provider and connection type if relevant, browser refresh/retry result,
approximate time and time zone, and a redacted screenshot. Email **contact@evecta.io** and never
include credentials, OTPs, tokens, recovery codes, or full payment-card information.

# 21. Best Practices

## How can I write a strong lead-generation prompt?

Be specific about the target business, industry, location, and business need. Instead of "Find
companies that need marketing," use "Find independent restaurants in Kathmandu that may need
social media marketing and online reservation promotion."

## How can I make AI-generated emails more relevant?

Provide a clear campaign intent: exact service/product, target audience, problem being solved,
main benefit, preferred tone, and desired next step. Also review each generated email before
sending.

## How many industries should I target in one campaign?

Generally it's better to create separate campaigns per industry or customer group — hotels,
schools, restaurants, and software companies typically have different needs and shouldn't receive
the same campaign message.

## What should I check before launching a campaign?

Confirm the selected leads are relevant, the campaign intent is clear, emails are personalized,
names and business details are correct, Spam Check results are acceptable, the sending mailbox is
connected, the schedule is correct, the call to action is clear, and credits/sending capacity are
available. Also double-check the lead list, since leads cannot be added once the campaign is
launched.

## How should I use Campaign Insights?

Understand who opened, replied, or didn't engage — but don't judge performance only by open rate.
Compare lead quality, reply quality, campaign intent, subject line, email content, sending time,
and mailbox health.

## How can I avoid generic outreach?

Use narrower target groups, a specific campaign intent, explain the actual customer problem, and
review AI-generated drafts before sending. Avoid one broad campaign for unrelated industries.

## Does more email volume always produce better results?

No. High volume without relevant targeting and strong personalization can lead to poor
engagement, spam complaints, and reduced mailbox reputation. Relevant prospects, clear messaging,
safe sending, and thoughtful manual outreach matter more than volume alone.

## Should I use my main business email for cold outreach?

This depends on mailbox reputation, sending strategy, risk tolerance, and company policy. Some
users prefer a separate warmed mailbox to protect their primary communication address from
outreach-related deliverability risks.

fully process 25 leads. **Decide:** raise Free to 100 Email Generation credits, lower Free Lead
credits to 25, or document the limitation explicitly.

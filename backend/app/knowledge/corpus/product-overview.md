# What Evecta is and who it is for

## Who are you? What are you? What do you do?

I am the Evecta assistant. I find business leads for you and answer questions about how Evecta works.

## What are you specialized in? What can you help me with? What services do you provide? What features do you have? What can you do for us?

I am specialized in B2B lead generation and cold email outreach on Evecta. I can help you:

- **Find leads** — describe the businesses you want and I will find matching prospects.
- **Manage leads** — save prospects to your Lead Database and revisit past searches.
- **Write outreach** — generate a personalized email for each prospect from your campaign intent.
- **Check deliverability** — run a Spam Check before you send.
- **Send campaigns** — through your own connected mailbox or an Evecta warmed address.
- **Handle replies** — read and answer prospect replies in the Unified Inbox.
- **Track results** — see who opened, replied, bounced, or did not open.
- **Answer product questions** — pricing, credits, limits, setup, and troubleshooting.

## What is Evecta?

Evecta is an AI-powered B2B lead generation and email outreach platform. It helps users find relevant businesses, save and manage leads, generate personalized emails, check email content for spam risks, send campaigns through connected or warmed mailboxes, manage replies, and track campaign performance from one platform.

## What problem does Evecta solve?

Evecta combines lead discovery, lead management, AI-personalized email writing, spam checking, mailbox integration, campaign management, and campaign insights into a single workflow. Without Evecta, businesses may need separate tools for lead research, email writing, spam testing, campaign sending, inbox management, and analytics.

Its core features are:

- **AI Lead Generation:** Describe the businesses you want to target by industry, market, location, or customer profile, and Evecta generates relevant business prospects.
- **Permanent Search History:** Every lead search is permanently stored, including saved and discarded results.
- **Lead Database:** Save selected prospects to a cleaner active list. Unlike Search History, leads here can be deleted.
- **Campaign Creation:** Create campaigns directly from Search History, the Lead Database, or other lead views.
- **AI-Personalized Email Generation:** A personalized email draft per lead, based on the campaign objective and that prospect's business context — not just swapping the recipient's name.
- **Spam Check:** Analyze a message for spam and deliverability risks before sending.
- **Mailbox Integration:** Connect your own email account or use Evecta's warmed mailboxes.
- **Campaign Management:** Select leads, review AI-generated drafts, schedule outreach, and monitor activity.
- **Unified Inbox:** Read and respond to prospect replies inside Evecta.
- **Campaign Insights:** Opens, replies, bounces, unopened emails, and overall campaign activity.
- **Credits and Packages:** Monthly packages plus additional credits when more usage is needed.

## How does Evecta work?

The typical workflow is: describe the target audience, generate leads, review Search History, save selected leads to the Lead Database, create a campaign, generate personalized emails, run spam checks, send through a connected mailbox, track engagement, manage replies in the Unified Inbox, and use Campaign Insights to improve future campaigns.

## Who are the target users and customers?

Evecta is built for businesses and professionals who rely on outbound sales and want a faster way to find customers and manage outreach. It is ideal for:

- **Startup Founders:** Founders looking to acquire their first customers.
- **Small & Medium Businesses (SMBs):** Businesses that want to grow without building a large sales team.
- **Sales Teams & Business Development Representatives (BDRs):** Teams generating leads and managing outbound campaigns.

Whether a user is looking for their first client or running thousands of outreach emails a month, Evecta keeps the entire process in one place.

## Is Evecta only a lead-generation tool?

No. Lead generation is only the first part of Evecta. It also provides lead management, AI-personalized email writing, automatic spam checking, mailbox integration, warmed sending addresses, campaign creation, campaign scheduling, reply management, and campaign-level insights.

## Can Evecta replace multiple sales and outreach tools?

Evecta can replace several tools used during the B2B outreach process by combining lead generation, email personalization, spam checking, campaign management, mailbox connection, reply handling, and campaign insights in one platform. Some businesses may still use separate CRM, sales calling, or advanced email-verification tools depending on their internal workflow.

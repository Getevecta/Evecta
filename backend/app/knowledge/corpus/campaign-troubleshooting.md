# Campaign and email generation troubleshooting

## Why can I not create a campaign?

Confirm at least one valid saved lead is selected, required campaign information is complete, a sending mailbox is available, sufficient credits and usage allowance are available, and the account has the required permission.

## Why can I not create a campaign from selected leads?

Confirm that at least one valid saved lead is selected and that the campaign name and required setup information are complete. If selection came from another view, return to that view and select the leads again. Refresh once if the selection did not carry into the Draft campaign.

## Why is a campaign still Draft?

Draft is correct until the campaign is launched. Complete lead selection, campaign intent, mailbox selection, email generation, Spam Check and review, test preview if desired, and launch readiness. "Scheduled" is not a stored status.

## Why was my email not generated?

Possible causes: missing or unclear campaign intent, incomplete lead information, insufficient Email Generation credits, a temporary AI-processing error, or a network or server issue. Improve the campaign intent and regenerate.

## Why did AI email generation partially fail or stop?

Check the generation status and failed count. Generation can be cancelled, resumed, or discarded. Resume interrupted work when appropriate, regenerate failed or individual emails, and review successful drafts. Do not launch until the intended messages are ready and approved.

## Why did editing an email make it require review or Spam Check again?

Editing or regenerating changes the content that was previously reviewed. The email returns to the appropriate pending or compliance state so the changed version can be checked and approved before sending.

## Why is an email rejected or excluded from launch?

Rejected emails are intentionally skipped. Review the content and lead context, edit or regenerate if the message should be reconsidered, rerun required checks, and approve only the final acceptable version.

## Why is the campaign not ready to launch?

Open the launch-readiness checklist. Common blockers are missing leads, incomplete generation, pending email review, failed required checks, no approved emails, disconnected or unselected mailbox, insufficient Outreach credits, or another incomplete campaign setting.

## Why did the test email fail?

Confirm that the user profile has an email address, the campaign has previewable content, and the sending setup is available. If the error says the user email is missing, update the profile email and retry. A test failure does not launch the campaign.

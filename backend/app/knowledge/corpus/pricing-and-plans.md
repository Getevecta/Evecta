# Plans and pricing

This page covers how much Evecta costs, what the price of each plan is, and how much you pay per month.

Evecta offers four plans: Free, Starter, Growth, and Scale. Each plan includes Lead Credits, Email Generation Credits, and Outreach Credits. Email Generation Credits are used for both AI email drafts and Spam Checks.

Credits are not the only limit on sending. Outreach credits set how many emails a plan allows, but actual sending is also capped at 50 successful campaign emails per UTC day, with at least 60 seconds between sends. Both limits apply.

## What is included in the Free plan?

The Free plan costs $0/month and is available for everyone to try the platform.

- Lead Credits: 50
- Email Generation Credits: 50
- Outreach Credits: 50
- Lead Search: Basic targeted search
- Verified Lead Data: Limited access
- AI-Assisted Drafts: Professional and Friendly tones
- Campaign Usage: Test outreach campaigns
- Open & Reply Tracking: Track opens and replies
- Campaign Dashboard: Campaign performance dashboard
- Platform Access: Essential platform access

## What is included in the Starter plan?

The Starter plan costs $29/month and is designed for individuals and small teams.

- Lead Credits: 500/month
- Email Generation Credits: 1,000/month + 250 one-time bonus credits
- Outreach Credits: 1,500/month + 500 one-time bonus credits
- Lead Search: Targeted lead search
- Verified Lead Data: Verified lead access
- AI-Assisted Drafts: Professional & Friendly tones
- Campaign Usage: Run regular campaigns
- Open & Reply Tracking: Track opens and replies
- Campaign Dashboard: Campaign performance dashboard
- Platform Access: Core platform access

## What is included in the Growth plan?

The Growth plan costs $59/month, is Evecta's most popular plan, and is designed for growing businesses.

- Lead Credits: 1,150/month
- Email Generation Credits: 2,300/month + 500 one-time bonus credits
- Outreach Credits: 3,500/month + 1,000 one-time bonus credits
- Lead Search: Expanded lead search
- Verified Lead Data: More verified lead access
- AI-Assisted Drafts: Everything in Starter, plus Concise, Persuasive & Consultative tones
- Campaign Usage: Manage growing campaigns
- Open & Reply Tracking: Track opens and replies
- Campaign Dashboard: Campaign performance dashboard
- Platform Access: Complete platform access

## What is included in the Scale plan?

The Scale plan costs $119/month and is designed for teams managing outreach at scale.

- Lead Credits: 2,500/month
- Email Generation Credits: 5,000/month + 1,250 one-time bonus credits
- Outreach Credits: 7,500/month + 2,500 one-time bonus credits
- Lead Search: High-volume lead search
- Verified Lead Data: Large-scale verified lead access
- AI-Assisted Drafts: Everything in Growth, plus Research-Based, Problem-Focused & Strategic tones
- Campaign Usage: Run high-volume campaigns
- Open & Reply Tracking: Track opens and replies
- Campaign Dashboard: Campaign performance dashboard
- Platform Access: Complete platform access

## Which plan should I choose? How much does it cost? What is the price? How much do you charge?

It depends on outreach volume and needs:

- **Free** is best for trying out the platform before committing to a paid plan.
- **Starter** suits individuals and small teams sending regular but limited outreach.
- **Growth** suits growing businesses that need more credits, more drafting tones, and expanded lead search.
- **Scale** suits teams running high-volume outreach who need the largest credit allowance and full platform access.

On every plan, sending is capped at 50 successful campaign emails per UTC day regardless of how many Outreach credits the plan includes.

## Which AI email tones can I access on my plan?

Free and Starter users have access to the Professional and Friendly tones. Growth adds Concise, Persuasive, and Consultative. Scale adds Research-Based, Problem-Focused, and Strategic. Higher-tier tone groups remain locked according to the plan shown in the product.

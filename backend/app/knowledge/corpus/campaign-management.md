# Creating and managing campaigns

## How do I create a campaign in Evecta?

1. Select leads from Search History, generated results, or the Lead Database.
2. Create a new campaign.
3. Add the campaign name and intent.
4. Choose the sending mailbox.
5. Generate personalized emails.
6. Review the drafts.
7. Review the Spam Check results.
8. Schedule or launch the campaign.
9. Track campaign activity through Campaign Insights.

## Can I create campaigns from different sections?

Yes — including Search History and the Lead Database.

## Can I create unlimited campaigns?

Yes. The number of leads processed, emails sent, mailboxes available, or other usage may still depend on credits, sending capacity, and package conditions.

## Does unlimited campaigns mean unlimited emails?

No. Unlimited campaigns means unlimited campaign creation. Total emails and leads processed depends on available credits, mailbox limits, sending limits, or subscription conditions.

## Can I create separate campaigns for different industries?

Yes — recommended, since different audiences usually need different campaign intents and messaging.

## Can I review the emails before launching a campaign?

Yes. All drafts can be read, edited, approved, or rejected in the campaign email review step before launch.

## Can I edit an email inside a campaign?

Yes. Editing returns that email to a pending state so the changed version can be re-checked and approved before sending.

## Can I choose the mailbox used for a campaign?

Yes — an available connected mailbox or an eligible Evecta warmed email address.

## Can I use a different mailbox for each campaign?

Yes, provided the required mailboxes are connected or available under the account.

## Can I pause or stop a campaign once it has launched?

No. Once a campaign is launched, it cannot be paused or stopped.

## Can I duplicate an existing campaign?

No, there is currently no option to duplicate an existing campaign.

## Can I add leads after a campaign has started?

Leads can be added while the campaign is still in Draft. Once the campaign is launched, no new leads can be added — if a lead was missed after launch, a new campaign needs to be created for it.

## Can I remove leads before launching a campaign?

Yes, leads can be removed at any point while the campaign is still in Draft.

## Can I change a campaign after launch?

No. Draft editing is not available after launch: leads cannot be added, launched campaigns cannot be deleted, and the campaign cannot be paused or stopped. Create a new Draft campaign for materially different recipients or outreach.

## What are the actual campaign statuses?

Evecta stores only Draft, Ongoing, and Completed.

- **Draft** — setup and review are still in progress; the campaign has not been launched.
- **Ongoing** — the campaign was launched and still has sending work or active processing.
- **Completed** — campaign sending has finished.

"Scheduled" and "launched" describe actions or workflow moments; they are not stored campaign statuses.

## When is a campaign ready to launch?

It is ready when generation has finished, the required Spam Checks and compliance checks are complete, the emails intended for sending are approved, the selected mailbox is connected and eligible, and sufficient Outreach credits and sending capacity are available. Rejected emails are not sent.

## Can I send a test email before launching?

Yes. From the campaign preview or review flow, send a personalized test version to your own email address before launch. Use it to verify personalization, subject, layout, links, sender details, and the call to action. A test does not launch the campaign.

## Can I delete a campaign?

Drafted campaigns can be deleted, but launched campaigns cannot be deleted.

## Does my plan restrict AI email tone options?

Yes. In the "Generate with AI" draft flow, tone options are grouped by plan tier:

- **Free & Starter:** Professional, Friendly
- **Growth:** adds Concise, Persuasive, Consultative
- **Scale:** adds Research-Based, Problem-Focused, Strategic

Tones above the user's current plan appear greyed out with a lock icon and an "Upgrade to Unlock" badge.

## What pre-built templates are available under "Browse Templates"?

Three ready-made templates: Value Proposition, Network Outreach, and Professional — usable as an alternative to AI-generated drafts.

## Can I manage custom email templates?

Yes. In addition to the three ready-made templates, users can create, edit, delete, categorize, and favorite custom templates for reuse.

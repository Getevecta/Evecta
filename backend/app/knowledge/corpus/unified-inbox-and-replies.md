# Unified Inbox and replies

## Where can I see replies from prospects?

Inside Evecta's Unified Inbox.

## Can I reply to a prospect directly from Evecta?

Yes, using the connected mailbox.

## Do I need to open my external email account to respond?

No — campaign-related replies can be viewed and answered through the Unified Inbox.

## What can I do in the Unified Inbox?

Open a conversation to see its full thread, mark conversations as read, star important conversations, view the related campaign and lead context, and reply from inside Evecta using the connected mailbox.

## How do I know which campaign a reply belongs to?

Replies are linked with the relevant lead and campaign so the user has context before responding.

## How do I know which lead sent the reply?

The reply is connected to the relevant recipient information inside the Unified Inbox.

## What happens when a prospect replies?

The reply appears inside Evecta, allowing the user to review the conversation and respond directly.

## Does Evecta currently send automated follow-up sequences?

No. Automated follow-ups and sequencing are a future update, not a currently available feature. Users can continue the conversation manually by replying from the Unified Inbox.

## Can multiple team members manage replies?

Yes. There is currently no role-based restriction — whoever has access to the account that connected a given mailbox can use and manage that mailbox's inbox.

## Why is the Inbox empty?

Confirm that a mailbox is connected, the campaign sent from that mailbox, and recipients have replied to the connected address. Trigger or allow reply synchronization, clear restrictive filters, and check that replies were not sent elsewhere or filtered by the provider.

## Why is a reply not appearing in the Unified Inbox?

Possible causes: the mailbox is disconnected, mail synchronization is delayed, the response was sent to a different address, the message was filtered by the mailbox provider, the reply could not be linked to the campaign, or a temporary sync issue occurred.

## Why can I not open or reply to an Inbox thread?

The thread or lead may no longer be available to the account, the mailbox may be disconnected, or reply sending may have failed. Refresh, reopen the thread, verify mailbox connection, and retry without changing the recipient context. Escalate repeated "thread not found", authorization, or database errors.

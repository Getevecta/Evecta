# Spam Check

## What is Evecta's Spam Check?

It analyzes email content before sending and identifies language or patterns that may increase the risk of the email being treated as spam.

## Is the Spam Check automatic?

Emails generated for outreach go through Evecta's Spam Check as part of the preparation workflow.

## What does the Spam Check analyze?

It analyzes:

- Overly promotional language
- Spam-triggering words
- Excessive punctuation
- Misleading or exaggerated claims
- Poor content structure
- Suspicious links or formatting
- Other content-related deliverability risks

## Can I edit an email after the Spam Check?

Yes — and the email should be checked again after significant changes. Editing returns the email to a pending state so the changed version is re-checked before approval.

## What should I do if an email has a high spam risk?

Review the identified issues and revise the email — remove exaggerated claims, excessive sales language, unnecessary links, repeated punctuation, or misleading wording.

## Does passing the Spam Check guarantee inbox delivery?

No. It reduces content-related risk but does not guarantee inbox placement. Delivery also depends on mailbox reputation, domain configuration, sending volume, recipient provider, bounce rate, recipient engagement, and other factors.

## Why can an email still end up in spam after passing the Spam Check?

Because Spam Check focuses mainly on content. Other causes include poor sender reputation, a new or untrusted mailbox, missing SPF/DKIM/DMARC records, high sending volume, high bounce or complaint rates, recipient-server filtering, or low previous engagement.

## Does Evecta guarantee email replies?

No. Evecta helps improve targeting, personalization, sending preparation, and campaign tracking, but cannot guarantee opens, replies, meetings, or sales.

## Why did the Spam Check not complete?

Possible causes: the email draft was not generated, the content is empty, a temporary processing issue occurred, the request timed out, or the required credit or feature access is unavailable. Retry, and contact support if it continues.

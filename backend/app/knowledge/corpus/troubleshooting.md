# Troubleshooting lead search and saving

## Why is my lead search returning few results?

Possible reasons: the prompt is too narrow, the location has limited business data, too many conditions were included, the industry wording is unclear, or the requested business type is uncommon. Try a broader location, simpler industry description, or fewer restrictions.

## Why are the generated leads not relevant?

The prompt may be too broad or unclear. Add specifics on industry, location, business type, service requirement, and exclusions.

## Why can I not save a lead?

Check whether the lead is already saved, sufficient credits are available, the search result is fully loaded, the account has the required permission, or a temporary system error occurred. Refresh and retry; contact support if it continues.

## Why does AI Lead Generation say the message is empty or chat is starting up?

Enter a non-empty targeting request. If chat is starting up, wait briefly and retry once. A repeated startup or processing failure is a platform issue; preserve the non-secret error and time and contact support.

## How do I contact Evecta support?

Email contact@evecta.io. Support is available by email, with a response time within 24 hours.

## What is the final support checklist before escalation?

Record the account email, feature and screen, desired action, exact non-secret error, campaign or lead context, mailbox provider and connection type if relevant, browser refresh and retry result, approximate time and time zone, and a redacted screenshot. Email contact@evecta.io and never include credentials, OTPs, tokens, recovery codes, or full payment-card information.

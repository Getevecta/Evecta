# Security and privacy

## Does Evecta sell user data?

No. Evecta does not sell user or lead data.

## Who owns the campaigns and email content created in Evecta?

The user owns the campaigns, leads, and email content they create in Evecta.

## Can I delete my Evecta account?

There is currently no self-service account deletion option.

## How are mailbox credentials protected?

Mailbox credentials are encrypted.

## Does Evecta store my email password?

It depends on how the mailbox is connected:

- **Google/Gmail:** No. The connection uses Google authorization, so Evecta never asks for or stores the Google password.
- **Manual IMAP/SMTP:** Yes. The provider credentials or app password entered in the connection form are stored so Evecta can send and synchronize mail. They are held encrypted.

Never share passwords, app passwords, tokens, or recovery codes with support.

## Is it safe to share my mailbox password with Evecta support?

No. Never send a normal password, app password, OAuth token, recovery code, or other secret in chat or email. Google connections use Google authorization. Manual IMAP/SMTP credentials are handled separately by the product and should remain private.

## Who is responsible for lawful outreach and message claims?

The user is responsible for reviewing recipients, lawful basis or consent requirements, truthful claims, sender identity, required disclosures, and applicable email laws and provider policies. Evecta's AI generation and Spam Check assist preparation but do not provide legal approval or guarantee compliance.

## What should I do if data appears under the wrong user or campaign?

Stop the affected workflow and do not send. Record the screen, non-secret identifiers, time, and what appears incorrect, then email contact@evecta.io immediately. Do not modify or delete records merely to hide a possible account-association problem.

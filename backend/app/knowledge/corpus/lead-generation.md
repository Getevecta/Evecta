# Generating leads

## How do I generate leads in Evecta?

Enter a prompt describing the type of businesses you want to reach — industry, location, business type, service requirement, target market, or other relevant characteristics. Evecta uses this intent to generate a matching list of businesses.

## What should I include in a lead-generation prompt?

A strong prompt should include:

- The type of business you want to find
- The industry or service category
- The preferred location
- The type of customer you want to reach
- Any important business characteristics

Example: "Find hotels in Kathmandu." A more specific prompt generally produces more relevant results.

## Can I search for businesses by industry?

Yes — hotels, restaurants, schools, software companies, healthcare businesses, retailers, manufacturers, professional service providers, and more.

## Can I search for businesses by location?

Yes, by city. Result quality and availability may depend on how much business data exists for that location.

## Can I combine industry and location in one search?

Yes. Example: "Find digital marketing agencies in Sydney that work with real-estate companies."

## How does Evecta decide which businesses are relevant?

Evecta uses the meaning and intent of the prompt to identify businesses matching the requested industry, location, business type, or customer profile. Relevance depends partly on how clearly the target audience is described.

## What should I do if the generated leads are not relevant?

Refine the prompt with more specific information: exact industry, preferred location, business size, type of service offered, the type of problem the target business may have, and businesses that should be excluded.

## Are all generated leads automatically added to the Lead Database?

No. Generated leads first remain in Search History; the user decides which are useful and saves only those into the Lead Database.

## Can I generate leads without immediately creating a campaign?

Yes. Leads can be reviewed first, saved to the Lead Database, or revisited later via Search History.

## Can I create a campaign directly from generated leads?

Yes — from generated search results, Search History, or the Lead Database.

## How do I save generated leads?

Select the relevant generated results and save them to the Lead Database. Duplicate rules prevent the same user from saving the same email repeatedly. If a result is not saved, it remains available in its Search History entry.

## Why are some lead fields shown as N/A or still processing?

Business discovery and contact enrichment are separate. A business can be found even when an email, phone, address, industry, or website is unavailable or still being enriched. N/A means the value was not available; it should not be treated as verified contact data.

## What should I do while lead generation is still processing?

Keep the generation view open, or return through the active processing notification when available. Background enrichment can continue after initial businesses appear. Do not submit repeated identical searches merely because optional contact data is still processing.

## What happens if I leave the lead-generation page?

The interface may continue polling or allow the result to be reopened when processing completes. If unsaved results are present, follow the Save or Discard prompt before navigating so the intended leads are not lost from the active view. Search History retains the original search.

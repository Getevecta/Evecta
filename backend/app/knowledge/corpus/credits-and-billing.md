# Credits and billing

## How do Evecta credits work?

Evecta uses credits for specific product actions. The exact cost is shown before or when the action is performed. Credit use is split into three independent pools: Lead credits, Email Generation credits, and Outreach credits.

## Exactly how are credits calculated?

| Action | Cost |
| --- | --- |
| Generating one lead | 1 Lead credit |
| One AI email draft | 1 Email Generation credit |
| One Spam Check | 1 Email Generation credit |
| Sending one email | 1 Outreach credit |

The pools are independent — having credits in one pool does not satisfy an action that spends another pool.

## Is one lead equal to one credit?

Yes. Finding one lead uses 1 Lead credit.

## Do AI email generation and Spam Check use credits?

Yes. Each AI-generated email draft costs 1 Email Generation credit, and each Spam Check costs 1 Email Generation credit. Sending an email costs 1 Outreach credit.

## Are credits deducted again when I reopen an existing lead?

No, reopening a previously found or saved lead does not deduct another credit.

## Where can I see remaining credits and credit history?

Open Settings > Usage to review balances and consumption. The interface tracks Lead, Email Generation, and Outreach pools independently.

## Why does Evecta say I have insufficient credits when I still have credits?

Check the specific pool required by the action. Lead generation uses Lead credits; AI drafts and Spam Checks use Email Generation credits; sending uses Outreach credits. A positive balance in a different pool cannot be substituted.

## Do unused credits carry forward?

No. Unused monthly subscription credits expire and do not roll over to the next billing period.

## Do separately purchased top-up credits expire at subscription renewal?

No. Separately purchased top-up credits are stored independently and are not cleared when subscription credits renew. Subscription allowances follow their own billing-period rules.

## What happens when I run out of credits?

Once credits are finished, the user needs to upgrade their plan or purchase additional credits to continue using credit-based actions.

## Can I buy additional credits without upgrading my monthly plan?

Yes — additional credits can be purchased separately without changing the monthly subscription package.

## Can Free users buy top-up credits?

No. Credit top-ups are not available on the Free plan; upgrade to a paid plan first. Paid-plan top-up ranges and per-purchase caps depend on the selected pool and current plan.

## Why was my top-up amount rejected?

Select at least one credit pool, use an amount within the plan's allowed minimum and maximum, and use the required increment. The payment total must also meet the payment processor's minimum charge. Review the options shown in Billing rather than guessing an amount.

## Do I need to upgrade my package to get more credits?

No — credits can be added separately instead of upgrading the whole package. An upgrade may still be useful for additional recurring features, mailbox allowances, or monthly benefits.

## Are monthly packages the only payment option?

No — Evecta offers monthly packages and also allows separate additional-credit purchases.

## Why did my paid plan or top-up not appear after checkout?

Confirm that checkout completed and was paid, return to Evecta, and refresh Billing or Usage. Do not repeat payment immediately. If payment succeeded but the plan or credits remain missing, contact contact@evecta.io with the account email, checkout time, amount, and payment receipt or session reference. Never send full card details.

## How does subscription cancellation work? How do I cancel my subscription?

Use the available Billing or customer-portal cancellation flow. A subscription may remain active until the end of the paid period and show a scheduled cancellation date. If the product says the subscription is already cancelled, wait until the displayed expiry before attempting a conflicting subscription action.

## Why can I not open the billing portal?

The account needs an associated payment-customer record. Complete a subscription checkout first when instructed. Retry a temporary failure once, then contact support if the portal still cannot be created.

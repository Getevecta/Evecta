# Account access, onboarding and profile

## How do I create and verify an Evecta account?

Register with the requested account details, then enter the OTP sent to the registration email. If the OTP is invalid or expired, request a new code and use only the latest one. Evecta may require a short wait before another OTP can be sent.

## I already registered but cannot create another account with the same email. What should I do?

Sign in with the existing account. If the email exists but verification was not completed, use the verification flow and resend the OTP instead of registering again. Use Forgot Password if the password is unknown.

## What should I do if my OTP is invalid or expired?

Check that the code belongs to the correct email, enter the newest code exactly, and request a replacement after the resend wait period. Older codes may stop working when a newer code is issued. If repeated new codes fail, contact support without sharing the OTP.

## How do I reset a forgotten password?

Choose Forgot Password, enter the account email, request the reset OTP, verify the code, and set the new password. If the code is no longer valid, resend it and restart with the newest code.

## How do I change my password while signed in?

Open User Profile or Settings, choose the Password section, provide the required current and new-password information, and save. Reauthenticate if the session has expired.

## Can I update my profile and business details?

Yes. Open Profile or Settings to update personal and company information such as your name and business details. Keep this information accurate because available sender and company context can influence AI-personalized outreach content and signatures.

## Why does Evecta ask for business details during first-time setup?

New accounts without a company profile are prompted to complete business information. This helps provide accurate sender and company context for personalized outreach. The profile can be reviewed and updated later from User Profile or Settings.

## Why was I signed out or why did my session expire?

Sign in again. Sessions can expire or be revoked after logout, password changes, security changes, or an invalid refresh session. If login still fails, verify the email and password or use Forgot Password.

# Search History

## What is Search History in Evecta?

A permanent record of every lead search generated by the user, including results that were saved, discarded, or not selected.

## Are all previous lead searches stored in Search History?

Yes — every search remains available so the user can return to it later.

## Does Search History include discarded leads?

Yes. Discarded or unselected leads remain visible in the original search, so users can reconsider them later without re-running the search.

## Can I recover a lead that I previously discarded?

Yes — open the relevant Search History, locate the lead, and save it to the Lead Database. Discarding does not permanently remove it.

## Can Search History be deleted?

No. Search History is retained and cannot be deleted by the user, so previously generated results remain recoverable.

## Why is Search History permanent?

It prevents users from losing generated leads. A lead that isn't useful today may become relevant for a future service, campaign, location, or target market.

## Can I create a campaign from Search History?

Yes. Open the relevant search, select the leads you want, and create a campaign from that selection.

## Will deleting a lead from the Lead Database remove it from Search History?

No. Deleting removes the lead from the active saved-lead list only. Search History is permanent, so the original result remains there and the lead can be saved again later.

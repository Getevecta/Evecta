# Campaign Insights

## What are Campaign Insights?

Campaign-level and lead-level information about how recipients interacted with the outreach — who opened, who replied, and who did not open.

## What information can I see for each campaign?

Each campaign has its own insights page showing the full list of leads along with their status (sent, opened, replied). It also shows how many emails were sent and on which day, along with total opens and total replies for the campaign.

## How do I see who opened an email?

Open the relevant campaign and review the recipient activity or campaign insights section.

## How do I see who replied?

Open the relevant campaign and filter or review recipients with replied status, or check the Unified Inbox.

## How do I see which recipients did not open the email?

Open campaign insights and review recipients marked as not opened or unopened.

## Can I view activity for each lead?

Yes. Each lead in the campaign insights table shows its own status and activity.

## What does "Sent" status mean?

Evecta submitted the email through the selected sending mailbox. It does not necessarily mean the recipient opened, read, or replied.

## What does "Opened" status mean?

Email-open tracking detected the email was opened. Not always fully accurate — some providers block tracking, others load it automatically.

## What does "Replied" status mean?

A response from the recipient was detected and connected to the relevant campaign or conversation.

## What does "Not opened" status mean?

No open event has been detected. The email may genuinely be unopened, or the provider may have blocked open tracking.

## Are email-open results always accurate?

No — treat open tracking as a useful indicator, not an exact measurement. Some clients block tracking pixels; some privacy features auto-load images and create false opens.

## What should I do when a lead opens but does not reply?

Review the email content, offer, call to action, and relevance. A thoughtful manual reply or later outreach may be appropriate, but an open should not always be read as strong interest. Evecta does not currently automate follow-up sequences.

## What should I do when a lead does not open the email?

Review subject line, sending time, sender name, mailbox reputation, deliverability, and target relevance. A not-opened status should not always be interpreted as rejection. Any follow-up must currently be handled manually.

## How can Campaign Insights improve future campaigns?

They help identify which audiences engage more, which campaign intents perform better, which email approaches receive replies, and which subject lines may need improvement. Use these signals to plan appropriate manual next steps; automated follow-up sequences are not currently available.

## Can I compare campaigns?

Yes. Each campaign has its own insights page, so results can be reviewed side by side.

## Can I export campaign insights?

No, campaign insights currently cannot be exported.

## Does Evecta show a sending schedule for an active campaign?

Yes. Each launched campaign's insights page shows a "Daily Outreach Rate" (emails per day from the connected mailbox), a weekly bar chart (Sent, Open Rate, Reply Rate, Bounce Rate), an estimated completion date, and a per-lead status table for all leads in that campaign.

## Why are Campaign Insights not updating or appearing delayed?

Sending, delivery events, opens, bounces, and replies are processed asynchronously and need time to be received and processed. Refresh after allowing processing time. Open tracking may also be blocked or auto-triggered by recipient privacy features, so it is not exact.

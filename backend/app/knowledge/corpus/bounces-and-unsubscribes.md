# Bounces and unsubscribes

## What is an email bounce? What does bounced mean?

A bounce means a recipient's mail system did not accept or deliver the message. Common causes include an invalid or nonexistent address, a full mailbox, temporary server problems, domain or DNS issues, provider filtering, sender-reputation problems, or policy restrictions.

## Where can I see Bounce Rate, and what should I do about bounces?

Bounce Rate appears in the campaign insights weekly performance chart. Review bounced recipients, correct or remove invalid addresses, avoid retrying permanently invalid recipients, improve list quality, and check mailbox and domain configuration. A high bounce rate can harm sender reputation.

## What should I do with a permanently bounced address?

Correct it only when a verified replacement address is available; otherwise remove or suppress it from future outreach. Repeatedly sending to permanently invalid recipients damages list quality and sender reputation.

## Does Evecta add unsubscribe links?

Yes. Campaign email handling supports an unsubscribe link and one-click unsubscribe so recipients can opt out.

## What happens after a recipient unsubscribes?

The lead is marked opted out or unsubscribed and should not be emailed again. Treat the opt-out as final unless the recipient later gives valid new consent; do not add that lead to another outreach campaign.

## What should I do with an unsubscribed lead?

Do not email the lead again through another campaign or mailbox. Keep the opt-out suppression intact unless valid new consent is obtained outside this workflow. Unsubscribe handling takes precedence over campaign intent or remaining credits.

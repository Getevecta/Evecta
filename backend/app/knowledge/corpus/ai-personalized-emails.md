# AI-personalized emails

## How does Evecta generate personalized emails?

The user provides the campaign's purpose or intent. Evecta uses that intent, plus available information about each lead, to generate a separate outreach email per prospect.

## Does Evecta send the same email to every lead?

No. Each lead gets an individual draft. All emails follow the same campaign objective but are adjusted to the particular prospect and available business information.

## What does campaign intent mean?

The main goal and direction of the outreach — what's being offered, who the user wants to reach, why the offer may be relevant, what action the recipient should take, and what tone the email should use.

## Why is campaign intent important?

It guides the AI, helping produce messages that stay focused on the same objective while still being personalized per lead.

## What information should I include in the campaign intent?

Include:

- The product or service being offered
- The target audience
- The main customer problem
- The value or benefit being presented
- The preferred tone
- The desired call to action

Example: "Introduce our website redesign service to hotels in Kathmandu. Focus on improving direct bookings and mobile experience. Keep the tone professional and invite them to a short consultation."

## Are AI-generated emails different for every lead?

Yes — separate content per prospect, aligned to the same campaign intent.

## Can I review an AI-generated email before sending?

Yes. Users should confirm content, offer, business details, tone, and call to action before sending.

## Can I edit an AI-generated email?

Yes. Open the draft in the campaign email review step and edit it directly. Editing returns the email to a pending state so the changed version can be re-checked and approved before sending.

## Can I regenerate an AI-generated email?

Yes. Improving the campaign intent before regenerating typically produces a more relevant result. Regeneration creates a new version for the selected email, which should be reviewed again.

## Can I change the tone of the email?

Yes — include the preferred tone (professional, friendly, concise, conversational, formal, consultative, and so on) in the campaign intent. Available tones depend on your plan.

## Can I create different campaigns for different services?

Yes — for different products, services, industries, locations, offers, or target audiences.

## What is the AI email review workflow?

Generate personalized per-lead drafts, run Spam Check, review and edit the messages, and regenerate an individual email when needed. Approve emails that are ready and reject those that should not be sent. If generation is interrupted, it can be resumed; generated emails can also be discarded when the user wants to restart the preparation step.

## What is the difference between cancel, discard, resume, and regenerate?

- **Cancel** stops the current email-generation run when supported.
- **Resume** continues an interrupted generation state.
- **Discard** removes the generated-email set so preparation can restart.
- **Regenerate** creates a new version for a selected email or requested generation scope.

Review the resulting version again after any of these.

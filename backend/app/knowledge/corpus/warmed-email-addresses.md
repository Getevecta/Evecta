# Warmed email addresses

## What are Evecta's built-in warmed email addresses?

Sending mailboxes that have undergone a gradual warm-up process before being used for outreach, helping establish more natural sending activity and healthier sender reputation than a completely new address.

## Why should I use a warmed email address?

A newly created mailbox may have little or no sender reputation. A properly warmed mailbox can reduce some deliverability risks tied to sending outreach from a brand-new address.

## Does a warmed mailbox guarantee inbox delivery?

No. Email quality, targeting, sending behaviour, domain setup, bounce rate, complaints, and recipient engagement still affect deliverability.

## Are warmed email addresses included in every plan?

Yes, warmed email addresses are available in all packages. Each package comes with a different number of leads and outreach credits, so how much warmed-mailbox outreach a user can send depends on their package's credit allowance.

## Can I choose between my own mailbox and a warmed mailbox?

Yes.

## What is the difference between my own mailbox and a warmed one?

Both send campaign emails from inside Evecta and both are subject to the same limits. They differ in setup, branding, and starting reputation.

**Your own mailbox**
- Setup: connect it yourself through Google/Gmail authorization or manual IMAP/SMTP.
- Branding: emails come from your own domain, so recipients recognize your company.
- Reputation: it carries whatever sending reputation the mailbox already has, good or bad.
- Best when brand identity matters or the mailbox already has healthy sending history.

**An Evecta warmed mailbox**
- Setup: none — warmed addresses are available on all packages and ready to use.
- Branding: the address is not on your own domain, so it does not carry your company's name.
- Reputation: it has been gradually warmed before use, giving healthier standing than a brand-new address.
- Best when you don't want outreach sent from your primary mailbox, or you need a ready-to-use sending option.

**The same for both:** 50 successful campaign emails per UTC day, at least 60 seconds between sends, and your package's Outreach credit allowance. Neither option raises the daily cap, and neither guarantees inbox delivery.

## When should I use my own mailbox?

When brand identity matters, the recipient should recognize the company domain, existing business communication already happens through that mailbox, or the mailbox has a healthy sending reputation. Choosing your own mailbox does not change how much you can send: the 50-per-UTC-day cap and the 60-second spacing apply to every mailbox on every plan.

## When should I use a warmed mailbox?

When the user doesn't want to send outreach from their primary mailbox, the existing mailbox is new or unprepared for outreach, a separate address is wanted for campaigns, or a ready-to-use sending option is needed.

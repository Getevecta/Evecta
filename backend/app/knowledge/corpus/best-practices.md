# Best practices for outreach

## How can I write a strong lead-generation prompt?

Be specific about the target business, industry, location, and business need. Instead of "Find companies that need marketing," use "Find independent restaurants in Kathmandu that may need social media marketing and online reservation promotion."

## How can I make AI-generated emails more relevant?

Provide a clear campaign intent: exact service or product, target audience, problem being solved, main benefit, preferred tone, and desired next step. Also review each generated email before sending.

## How many industries should I target in one campaign?

Generally it's better to create separate campaigns per industry or customer group — hotels, schools, restaurants, and software companies typically have different needs and shouldn't receive the same campaign message.

## What should I check before launching a campaign?

Confirm the selected leads are relevant, the campaign intent is clear, emails are personalized, names and business details are correct, Spam Check results are acceptable, the sending mailbox is connected, the schedule is correct, the call to action is clear, and credits and sending capacity are available. Also double-check the lead list, since leads cannot be added once the campaign is launched.

## How should I use Campaign Insights?

Understand who opened, replied, or didn't engage — but don't judge performance only by open rate. Compare lead quality, reply quality, campaign intent, subject line, email content, sending time, and mailbox health.

## How can I avoid generic outreach?

Use narrower target groups, a specific campaign intent, explain the actual customer problem, and review AI-generated drafts before sending. Avoid one broad campaign for unrelated industries.

## Does more email volume always produce better results?

No. High volume without relevant targeting and strong personalization can lead to poor engagement, spam complaints, and reduced mailbox reputation. Relevant prospects, clear messaging, safe sending, and thoughtful manual outreach matter more than volume alone.

## Should I use my main business email for cold outreach?

This depends on mailbox reputation, sending strategy, risk tolerance, and company policy. Some users prefer a separate warmed mailbox to protect their primary communication address from outreach-related deliverability risks.

# The Lead Database

## What is the Lead Database?

The user's selected collection of useful prospects. Unlike Search History, which keeps every generated result permanently, it contains only the leads the user intentionally saves — and those can be removed.

## What is the difference between Search History and the Lead Database?

Search History keeps all generated searches (saved and discarded) and is retained permanently. The Lead Database contains only selected and saved leads, and those can be deleted.

## Why should I use the Lead Database if Search History already stores leads?

Search History is a permanent archive of every search. The Lead Database is a cleaner, active list containing only the prospects the user currently wants to manage, organize, or contact.

## Can I delete leads from the Lead Database?

Yes. Deleting a saved lead does not remove the original result from Search History.

## Can I save a lead from Search History to the Lead Database?

Yes — open the relevant search, select the lead, and save it.

## Can I create a campaign from the Lead Database?

Yes. Select the saved leads you want to target and create a campaign from that selection.

## What can I do with a saved lead?

Open the lead, review its details and campaign context, update editable information, star or unstar it, select it for a campaign, or delete it from the Lead Database. Deleting the saved lead does not remove its original Search History result.

## Why was a generated or imported lead skipped as a duplicate?

Evecta normalizes email addresses and checks the user's existing Lead Database as well as duplicates within the current import. Review the existing saved lead instead of creating another copy.

## Why can I not find a Lead Database entry?

Clear restrictive search or filters, check the relevant page, and verify whether the lead was saved rather than only generated. Review Search History for the original result. The lead may also have been deleted from the active database.

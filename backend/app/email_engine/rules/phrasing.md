# Phrasing guidance. Two roles:
#   sentence_patterns + tone_hygiene -> ALWAYS applied (baseline quality, every email)
#   rhythm_variants                  -> the variation engine picks ONE per email, so the
#                                       overall feel varies across leads (this is the
#                                       "variation engine changes phrasing patterns" axis)

# Always applied — baseline quality rules.
sentence_patterns:
  - "Vary sentence length: mix one short punchy sentence with one longer one."
  - "Avoid starting consecutive sentences with the same word (I, We, Our)."
  - "Prefer active voice over passive."
  - "Lead with the recipient (you/your) more than the sender (I/we)."
  - "Use specific, concrete language over vague marketing adjectives."
  - "Avoid filler openers like 'I just wanted to reach out' or 'quick question'."
tone_hygiene:
  - "Sound like a real person, not a brochure."
  - "No corporate jargon or buzzword stacking."
  - "Avoid hyperbole and superlatives (best, leading, world-class, revolutionary)."
  - "One idea per paragraph; keep paragraphs to 1-3 sentences."
  - "Avoid repeating the same value phrase twice in one email."

# One picked per email by the variation engine — nudges the overall rhythm/feel.
rhythm_variants:
  - "Favor short, direct sentences; keep it crisp and minimal."
  - "Use a slightly more conversational, flowing rhythm."
  - "Balance one vivid, specific detail with otherwise plain, clear language."
  - "Keep it tight and skimmable: short paragraphs, no filler."

# Phrasing & Sentence-Pattern Guidance

Applied on top of whichever structure is selected, so a campaign's emails feel individually
written rather than stamped from one mold.

- **`sentence_patterns` + `tone_hygiene`** are baseline quality rules — every email gets all
  of them.
- **`rhythm_variants`** is a variation axis — the engine picks **one** per email to shift the
  overall feel. This is what makes phrasing genuinely differ lead-to-lead.
- Keep rhythm variants about *feel/rhythm only* — don't restate greeting, structure, CTA, or
  closing rules here (those live in their own files).

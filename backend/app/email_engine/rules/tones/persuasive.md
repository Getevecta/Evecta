# Tone: Persuasive

**Essence:** Confident and action-oriented — motivates the reader to care and respond without
sounding pushy or salesy. Clear value, a credible reason to act, and a direct but respectful ask.

**Do:**
- Lead with a sharp, relevant value proposition tied to the recipient's world.
- Use confident, active language and a clear next step.
- Keep momentum — short sentences, purposeful flow, one primary call to action.

**Don't:**
- Stack hype, urgency tricks, or spam trigger words (free, act now, guaranteed).
- Sound arrogant or overpromise outcomes you cannot support.
- Bury the ask or add multiple competing calls to action.

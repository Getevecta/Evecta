# Tone: Problem-Focused

**Essence:** Pain-point led outreach — name a challenge the recipient likely faces, show you
understand it, then connect your offer as a relevant fix. Energetic but grounded in their reality.

**Do:**
- Open with a specific problem, friction, or missed opportunity in their industry or role.
- Bridge naturally from the pain to how you can help — concrete, not generic.
- Stay upbeat and forward-looking while remaining credible and concise.

**Don't:**
- Invent problems you cannot infer from context or merge fields.
- Use fear-mongering, exaggerated doom, or manipulative urgency.
- Let energy override clarity — every sentence should earn its place.

# Tone: Partnership-Focused

**Essence:** Frames the message as a mutual fit between equals, not a sales pitch. Emphasizes
collaboration, shared value, and a long-term relationship over an immediate transaction.

**Do:**
- Speak peer-to-peer; position both sides as bringing something to the table.
- Highlight mutual benefit and how a relationship could work.
- Stay respectful, balanced, and low-pressure.

**Don't:**
- Sound like a vendor pitching a buyer.
- Over-promise or lead with your own offering before the mutual angle.
- Use pushy or transactional language.

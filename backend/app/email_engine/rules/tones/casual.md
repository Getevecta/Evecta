# Tone: Casual

**Essence:** Light, simple, and human — like a quick, low-key note from one person to
another. The most relaxed tone, but still respectful and clear.

**Do:**
- Keep it short, plain, and jargon-free.
- Use everyday language and contractions.
- Get to the point quickly; favor brevity over polish.

**Don't:**
- Use corporate-speak or formal flourishes.
- Slip into slang or text-speak that reads as unprofessional.
- Add emojis or exclamation-heavy hype.

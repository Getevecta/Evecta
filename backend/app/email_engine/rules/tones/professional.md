# Tone: Professional

**Essence:** Formal, polished, and respectful — suited to first-touch B2B outreach to
someone the sender hasn't met. Confident and clear without being stiff or salesy.

**Do:**
- Use complete, grammatically clean sentences.
- Keep vocabulary clear and appropriate to the recipient's industry.
- Stay measured and courteous; let the value speak for itself.
- Light contractions are fine ("I'm", "you're") — they keep it human.

**Don't:**
- Use slang, exclamation points, or over-familiar openers ("Hey!", "What's up").
- Add emojis or manufactured enthusiasm.
- Sound stiff or bureaucratic — professional is not the same as cold.

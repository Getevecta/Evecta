# Tone: Soft Introduction

**Essence:** A gentle, no-pressure first touch. The goal is simply to introduce the sender
and open a door — not to sell. Brief, courteous, and very easy to reply to.

**Do:**
- Keep it short and unintrusive; respect the reader's time.
- Make the ask the lowest-commitment option possible.
- Be courteous and humble in framing the outreach.

**Don't:**
- Push for a meeting or decision in this first message.
- Stack multiple asks or a long value pitch.
- Use any urgency or pressure language.
